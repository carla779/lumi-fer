STEP 3: Trim to 15–25 seconds around the surprise reveal
---------------------------------------------------------
Use FFmpeg to trim each clip to the key surprise moment:

  ffmpeg -i SUR_01_ShinLim_AGT.mp4 -ss 00:03:10 -t 00:00:25 -c copy SUR_01_raw.mp4

Watch each one and note when the biggest "reveal" or "impossible
moment" happens, then trim to 15–25 seconds centred on that moment.
